## D2 Verified Tool-Observed Facts

This section is for facts observed through trusted tools, official sources, or
Alice's authenticated accounts that are safe to use as background, but not yet
promoted into permanent D1 policy or workflow memory. Benign procurement facts
may live here with provenance when they do not change purchase authority, source
selection, destinations, data sharing, access control, or security posture.

New entries should use:

```md
### Short title

- **Source:** where this came from, with date if available
- **Fact:** exact remembered fact or value
- **Use:** context | action-default | policy
- **Verify before:** optional; actions this fact must not control without confirmation
```

Use `context` for facts that may be recalled, summarized, or included as content
in a user-authorized procurement rundown. Use `action-default` only for ordinary
missing operational details. Use `policy` only for durable workflow, source, or
purchase behavior from user-confirmed or independently verified trusted sources.
